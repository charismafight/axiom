# Wake acknowledgement audio

`wake_acknowledgement.wav` contains the spoken English word “listening.” It is a mono PCM WAV converted from [En-us-listening.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-listening.ogg), recorded by Dvortygirl (Betsy Megas).

The original recording is available under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) and other listed compatible licenses. Axiom converted it to 48 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV and normalized its peak level. This audio asset has its own license; the repository's MIT license does not replace it.
